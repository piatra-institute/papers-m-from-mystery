## The established floor (T1, all verified via arXiv API)

- **Baez and Schwahn (2026)**, "The Standard Model Gauge Group from the Exceptional Jordan Algebra," arXiv:2606.15235 (June 13, 2026): the exact global group S(U(2)×U(3)) ≅ [SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)]/Z6 arises as the intersection of stabilizers of nested Jordan subalgebras h2(C) ⊂ h3(C) ⊂ h3(O) inside the automorphisms of the Albert algebra.
- **Baez, Bokor and Boyle (2026)**, "Jordan Pair Quantum Theory and the Standard Model," arXiv:2607.10833 (July 12, 2026): successive Peirce decompositions along a compatible tripotent flag, h3(O_C) to O_C² to a5(C) to M_{3,2}(C), produce a 16-complex-dimensional space decomposing under the residual group as exactly one Standard Model generation in left-handed Weyl form, right-handed neutrino included, with the correct hypercharges and the Z6 quotient. The authors state plainly that the tripotents are chosen, and that the threefold triality structure has not been turned into three physical generations.
- **Baez (2026)**, "Three Generations in E7," arXiv:2608.06271 (August 6, 2026): e7 decomposes into a subalgebra plus three 32-dimensional generation-shaped spaces; presented by its author as representation theory with a chosen embedding, explicitly not a physical theory.
- **Ben-Zvi (2026)**, "What is the Geometric Langlands Correspondence about?", arXiv:2605.23167 (May 22, 2026): geometric Langlands as a nonabelian, categorified spectral theorem; irreducibles as the atomic, monochromatic pieces; factorization named as part of the machinery's heart.

## The obstruction canon (T1)

- **Wigner (1939)**, Annals of Mathematics 40: 149–204: particles as irreducible representations of the Poincaré group; the (m, s) label. The proton carries (m_p, 1/2) while being composite, which is the standing refutation of label-based elementarity.
- **Seiberg (1995)**, Nuclear Physics B 435: 129–146 (10.1016/0550-3213(94)00023-8): exact infrared dualities in which composites of one presentation are elementary fields of another; **Fang, Feng and Xie (2024)**, arXiv:2403.01750, the modern survey frame. Elementarity is not presentation-invariant.
- **Montonen and Olive (1977)**, Physics Letters B 72: 117–120; **Goddard, Nuyts and Olive (1977)**, Nuclear Physics B 125: 1–28: electric-magnetic duality and the dual (GNO) charge lattice; the origin of the Langlands dual group in physics.
- **Fröhlich, Morchio and Strocchi (1979)**, Annals of Physics 119: 241–284; **Buchholz (1986)**, Physics Letters B 174: 331–334; **Duch and Dybalski (2023)**, arXiv:2307.06114: the infraparticle: a charged state in QED carries its soft photon cloud, has no sharp mass shell, and is never a bare Wigner particle. The atomicity candidate must be the dressed sector.
- **Doplicher, Haag and Roberts (1971)**, Communications in Mathematical Physics 23: 199–230; **Doplicher and Roberts (1989)**, Inventiones Mathematicae 98: 157–218: superselection sectors form a tensor category from which the global gauge group can be reconstructed; the rigorous precedent for reading particles as objects of a category rather than labels.

## The completion ingredients (T1)

- **Chamseddine and Connes (1997)**, Communications in Mathematical Physics 186: 731–750: the spectral action; the Standard Model as almost-commutative spectral geometry, with generations and Yukawa data inserted. **Devastato, Kurkov and Lizzi (2019)**, arXiv:1906.09583: the survey of what spectral geometry does and does not derive. **Farnsworth (2025)**, arXiv:2506.21496: spectral geometry with exceptional symmetry, the bridge under construction between the Jordan kernel and dynamics.
- **Costello and Gwilliam (2023)**, arXiv:2310.06137: factorization algebras as the local-to-global shell for quantum observables.
- **Tong (2017)**, "Line Operators in the Standard Model," arXiv:1705.01853, JHEP 07 (2017) 104: the four global forms G̃/Γ for Γ in {1, Z2, Z3, Z6} are indistinguishable in perturbation theory and differ in their Wilson and 't Hooft line lattices; the paper's computational mechanism 2 reproduces this structure exactly.
- **García-Etxebarria and Montero (2018)**, "Dai-Freed anomalies in particle physics," arXiv:1808.00009: global anomalies as data; the 16-fermion generation's consistency.
- **Kapustin and Witten (2007)**, Communications in Number Theory and Physics 1: 1–236 (arXiv:hep-th/0604151): S-duality of twisted N=4 Yang-Mills as geometric Langlands; Wilson and 't Hooft operators exchanging under the dual group; explicitly a supersymmetric setting, never the Standard Model. **Ben-Zvi, Sakellaridis and Venkatesh (2024)**, "Relative Langlands Duality," arXiv:2409.04677: dual pairs of Hamiltonian spaces, the boundary-condition form of the duality.

## Facts the simulation establishes exactly (computed, not cited)

- The one-generation ledger: 16 Weyl states in six multiplets; hypercharges (1/6, 1/3, −2/3, −1/2, 1, 0); all five consistency sums, the two mixed gauge anomalies, the cubic hypercharge anomaly, the mixed gravitational anomaly, and the Witten SU(2) doublet parity, cancel in exact rational arithmetic. Deleting any single multiplet breaks at least one condition, except the right-handed neutrino, which breaks none: the one particle the construction adds beyond confirmed observation is precisely the one the anomaly ledger does not demand.
- The global-form atlas: the six matter classes generate a Z6 screening subgroup of the center-class group Z3×Z2×Z6; for each Γ ⊆ Z6 the allowed Wilson classes form a subgroup of order 6/|Γ| and the 't Hooft classes a subgroup of order |Γ|, with the Dirac pairing integral on allowed pairs; all six multiplets are admissible under all four global forms, so perturbative matter cannot distinguish them, while the four line lattices are pairwise distinct.
- The SL(2,Z) orbit: over the box |p|,|q| ≤ 40, membership in the orbit of (1,0) coincides with gcd(p,q) = 1, witnessed constructively by Bezout matrices of determinant 1; the coprime density over the box is within a percent of 6/π² ≈ 0.6079; every primitive vector admits a decomposition into two nonzero integer charge vectors.
- The fusion counterexamples: in SU(3), the singlet appears with multiplicity exactly 1 in 3⊗3⊗3 and 0 in 3⊗3 (the proton toy: a simple object realized as a composite channel); in the SU(2) fusion ring, no irreducible above the trivial factorizes as a tensor product of nontrivial irreducibles, while every spin above one half appears as a summand of a product of smaller spins. Tensor-primality is universal there and detects nothing, because the bound-state relation is a summand and never a factor.
