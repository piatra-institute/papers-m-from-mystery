# M from Mystery

Exceptional Kernels, Global Charge Data, and the Electron as Atomos. What mathematical statement would mean that the electron is indivisible? Four obstructions define the problem: finite experimental resolution, the proton (irreducible label, composite carrier), exact dualities that exchange elementary fields with composites, and infrared dressing, which makes the physical electron an infraparticle rather than a bare Wigner state. The paper organizes these into a research programme whose established floor is new: in 2026 the exact Standard Model gauge group with its global Z6 quotient was derived as a stabilizer inside the automorphisms of the exceptional Jordan algebra, one full chiral generation was recovered from Peirce decompositions of a Jordan pair, and e7 was shown to contain three generation-shaped spaces. The paper defines the flagged Jordan-spectral kernel and its duality-completed quantum structure M, states emergence as a classification problem whose axioms may not hide the numbers 3, 2, 1, or 16, and computes everything on the floor exactly: the 16-state generation cancels all 5 anomaly conditions in exact rationals and is anomaly-rigid at every site except the right-handed neutrino, the one multiplet the ledger does not demand; the 4 global gauge forms are built as explicit line lattices, identical matter and 4 distinct theories, making the Jordan selection of the Z6 form a physical claim with a computable Langlands shadow; the SL(2,Z) orbit of the unit electric charge is verified to be the coprime vectors, every one of which still decomposes into nonzero constituents; and in the SU(2) fusion ring every irreducible is tensor-prime while every spin above one half is a summand of a product of smaller spins, so the bound-state relation is a summand and never a factor, and label-, simplicity-, and primality-based atomicity all fail computably. A 7-level hierarchy ending in duality-atomic and UV-atomic locates the electron conjecture, with the last loophole kept visible: even the strongest theorem would prove indivisibility within M, never beneath it.
